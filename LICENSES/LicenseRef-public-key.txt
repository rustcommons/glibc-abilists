The files in keys/ are OpenPGP public keys published by their holders (see
keys/keys.tsv for each key's holder and where it was obtained). They are
redistributed unchanged so that anyone can verify glibc's release-tag
signatures offline. No license is claimed for them by this project.

The organization release-signing public key at .github/release-signing-key.asc
is likewise redistributed unchanged from its holder. No license is claimed for
that public key by this project. It is separate from glibc's upstream tag keys.
